Confluence Easton is a new, free guide to downtown, for the folks who live here and the folks just visiting. It puts the events, the map, the shops and restaurants, the creatives and the artists all in one place, with plans for a day or a night out. Your listing is free and takes about five minutes.

What you get

  • A pin on the downtown mapPlus one-tap directions, so people can actually find your door.
  • A live OPEN signYour hours show open or closed in real time. No more "are they open?" calls.
  • Your events on the calendarShows, sales, classes, open studios. People save them to their plan and show up.
  • A spot in Day & Night guidesDate nights, kids' days and art walks send people from one stop to the next. Yours could be one of them.
  • Creatives get hiredPhotographers, designers, printers and musicians, listed right where local businesses go looking.
  • Artists get featuredEach week an artist gets the home screen, along with where to see or buy the work.

Good questions

Does it cost anything?

No. Listings are free, and nobody can pay to be featured. Guides and featured artists are picked for what makes a good day in Easton, and that's it.

Who is behind Confluence Easton?

Neighbors. It's an independent project built here in Easton, to make downtown easier to explore for the people who live here and the people passing through.

How do I change my listing later?

Send this form again with the same name and tell us what changed. We'll take care of it.

What are the "Example" listings in the app?

We're just getting started. The examples show how things will look, and they step aside as real listings come in.

Who sees my email and phone?

Only the person who reviews listings. We use them to check details with you, and they're never published.
