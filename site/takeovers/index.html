Good questions

How do I pay?

You don't pay to ask. We read every request, and if it's a fit you get an invoice. The spot is yours once it's paid.

Does a takeover change my free listing?

No. Your listing stays free and stays put. A takeover adds a spotlight on top of it for the week.

Will people know it's a takeover?

Yes. Every takeover carries a Takeover tag. Guides, the featured listings and the regular directory can't be bought.

What do artists send?

A wide piece for the top of the app (about 2400 by 1200 pixels, or a drawing we can scan), plus anything you want to lend the app that month: lettering, icons, little drawings. You keep the rights; we credit you everywhere it shows.

Can we say anything we want?

Almost. Keep it true and specific. No "best in town" claims, no hype words. We'll help it sound like Easton.
